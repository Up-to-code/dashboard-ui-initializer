# Sidebar Rules

The sidebar is the product's architecture. It should feel quiet, stable, and spatially reliable.

## Structure

- Product mark and workspace switcher at the top.
- Channel workspaces next.
- Core product areas after workspace context.
- Team/profile controls at the bottom.

## Sizing

- Expanded width: 264px.
- Collapsed width: 72px.
- Icon size: 16px to 18px.
- Item height: 36px to 40px.
- Group gap: 16px.

## Active State

Active items use a soft blue background, primary navy text, and a small blue indicator when needed. Avoid heavy fills.

## Channel Organization

Each channel should show:

- Channel icon or consistent glyph.
- Channel name.
- Connection state.
- Optional unread/action count.

## Collapse Behavior

Collapsed mode keeps icons, active state, tooltips, and workspace switching. Do not hide navigation depth behind unpredictable menus.
